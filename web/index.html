Finnish Food Scanner

Welcome to Food Scanner

This application helps you scan Finnish grocery products to get detailed information about ingredients, allergens, and recipe suggestions.

Key Features

🔍 Barcode Scanning

Scan product barcodes to get detailed nutritional information

⚠️ Allergen Warnings

Set up alerts for ingredients you want to avoid

🍳 Recipe Suggestions

Get recipe ideas based on scanned products

🧊 Inventory Integration

Connect with your virtual refrigerator and freezer inventory
